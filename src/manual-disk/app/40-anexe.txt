@doc instructiuni | anexe | Anexa 2: instructiunile I8086/I8088
  Anexa da formatul in cod masina al instructiunilor, cu mnemonicele lor,
  in grupe. Fiecare grupa trimite la paginile ei.

  {h}Formatul{/}  {d}exemplu: MOV, registru/memorie catre/de la registru{/}

  {d}┌─┬─┬─┬─┬─┬─┬─┬─┐ ┌─────┬─────┬─────┐{/}
  {d}│{/}1{d}│{/}0{d}│{/}0{d}│{/}0{d}│{/}1{d}│{/}0{d}│{/}{c}d{/}{d}│{/}{c}w{/}{d}│ │{/} mod {d}│{/} reg {d}│{/} r/m {d}│{/}
  {d}└─┴─┴─┴─┴─┴─┴─┴─┘ └─────┴─────┴─────┘{/}

   {c}d{/} = 1 "catre", 0 "de la"          {c}w{/} = 1 instructiune cuvint, 0 octet
   {c}s:w{/} = 01: 16 biti de date imediate; 11: 8 biti de date imediate
   {c}v{/} = 0: "count" = 1; 1: "count" in CL      {c}x{/} redundant
   {c}z{/}: primitive de sir, comparare cu ZF
   {c}AL{/} acumulator 8 biti  {c}AX{/} 16 biti  {c}CX{/} contor  {c}DX{/} port variabil
   {c}DS{/} segment de date  {c}ES{/} segment suplimentar

  {h}Prefixul de segment "override"{/}  {d}pag. 103{/}

  {d}┌─┬─┬─┬───┬─┬─┬─┐{/}
  {d}│{/}0{d}│{/}0{d}│{/}1{d}│{/}reg{d}│{/}1{d}│{/}1{d}│{/}0{d}│{/}   {c}reg{/}: {c}00{/} ES, {c}01{/} CS, {c}10{/} SS, {c}11{/} DS
  {d}└─┴─┴─┴───┴─┴─┴─┘{/}

   {b}Registrul                  Implicit  Cu prefix{/}
   IP {d}(adresa codului){/}        CS        niciodata
   SP {d}(adresa stivei){/}         SS        niciodata
   BP {d}(stiva, marker){/}         SS        BP+DS sau ES sau CS
   SI sau DI {d}(fara siruri){/}    DS        ES, SS sau CS
   SI {d}(sursa sirurilor){/}       DS        ES, SS sau CS
   DI {d}(destinatia sirurilor){/}  ES        niciodata
